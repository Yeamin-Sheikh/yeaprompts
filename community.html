Master prompt use guide

A Master Prompt is not a one-time prompt. It is a reusable AI system where character, camera style, story DNA, duration, audio, continuity, negative rules, and output format are already defined.

1
Open a new ChatGPT or Claude chat and paste the complete Master Prompt.
2
Type: Give me 10 topics.
3
Select your favorite idea: Topic 4.
4
Type: Make full prompt.
5
For modifications, do not paste the Master Prompt again. Give explicit instructions such as Keep everything the same, change the location, make the hook stronger, make it 15 seconds, or keep the same character.
6
For subsequent iterations, type Give me 10 more topics or Next video.
Core principle. The Master Prompt defines how to create. Your next command defines what to create.
