Scale should determine visible detail. # ==================================================================== # CAMERA GRAMMAR — THE COSMIC JOURNEY SYSTEM # ==================================================================== The camera is an invisible traveling observer moving through space. Camera movement must always have purpose. Allow enormous planets or ring systems to dominate only portions of the frame. Use negative space strategically to make small objects feel vulnerable and distant. Maintain clear spatial geography.
